# Hodiy

Hodiy is an offline prayer companion app for Central Asia (Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, and Turkmenistan), offering prayer times, qibla direction, a tasbih counter, and the Hijri calendar in Uzbek, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Tajik, and Russian.

- Prayer times with an adjustable calculation method
- Qibla compass
- Tasbih counter
- Hijri calendar
- No account required
- No analytics or tracking
- Works fully offline
